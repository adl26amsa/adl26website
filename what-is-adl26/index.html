What is ADL26

What is the AMSA National Convention?

TL;DR Australia’s premier medical student event.

The AMSA National Convention is the largest student-run medical conference in the Southern Hemisphere. Held annually, it brings together 1,000+ medical students from across Australia for a week-long event packed with:

  • World-class academic content: keynote speakers, clinical workshops, panels, and research.
  • Professional development: networking with leaders in medicine, policy, and advocacy.
  • Unforgettable social events: themed nights, gala balls, and bonding with future colleagues.

It’s the ultimate mix of learning, inspiration, and connection — shaping the next generation of medical leaders.

More than just a conference.

Academic Program

Inspiring keynotes, clinical workshops, panels, and cutting-edge medical content.

Social Events

Iconic themed nights, the gala ball, and networking in a fun, vibrant atmosphere.

Sports & Wellness

Get moving with inter-uni sports competitions, fitness challenges, and activities that keep your body and mind sharp.

Leadership & Connection

Opportunities to engage with advocacy, meet future colleagues, and shape the future of healthcare.

Adelaide 2026:
Convention Redefined

A bold new chapter for the AMSA National Convention.

Adelaide 2026 delivers a reimagined experience: fresh, ambitious, and grounded in the unique identity of South Australia. Expect cutting-edge academic content in iconic venues, immersive social events framed by the city’s natural beauty, and a community that feels more connected than ever before.

For over a decade, AMSA Convention has been where Australia’s future doctors come together to learn, lead, and celebrate. In 2026, we’re raising the bar with new opportunities for growth, wellness, and collaboration.

But, what’s in Adelaide?

Cultural Capital
Home to world-class food, arts, and festivals: all within walking distance.

Accessible & Affordable

Innovation Hub
A city driving health tech, research, and medical innovation in Australia.
