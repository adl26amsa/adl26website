Why attend?

The AMSA National Convention is Australia’s premier medical student conference, bringing together thousands of future doctors for a week that blends cutting-edge academic content with unforgettable social experiences. From inspiring keynote speakers and hands-on workshops to themed social nights and networking opportunities, it’s a celebration of everything that makes medical school more than just study.

It’s a week-long journey of medicine, connection, and student leadership: where future doctors come to be inspired, empowered, and united in shaping the future of healthcare.
